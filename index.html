03 · Product Case Study

From ambiguity to product decision

The case study shows the reasoning behind the prototype: user problem, research plan, metrics, prioritization, and experimentation.

Problem Research Journey Metrics Experiments Trade-offs
Problem framing

Users do not need “more onboarding.” They need clarity.

I framed the problem around three jobs-to-be-done: understand what is required, know what to do next, and see visible progress toward launch. That led to a narrower product hypothesis: contextual guidance can reduce uncertainty and prevent avoidable drop-off.

Research plan

Validate the friction before automating it.

For a real product, I would combine qualitative and behavioral evidence rather than relying on one source.

  • Customer interviews with recently activated and stalled providers
  • Support-ticket coding to identify repeat questions and unclear requirements
  • Funnel event analysis by onboarding step and cohort
  • Usability tests of the checklist and copilot interaction

Academic-project disclosure: this portfolio uses simulated interview notes and simulated analytics to demonstrate the methodology.

Journey design

Move from generic tasks to context-aware next actions.

The proposed experience uses provider type, stage, location, urgency, and blocker context. Instead of showing a long checklist, it highlights the highest-priority action, explains why it matters, then reveals the next step.

Success definition

Measure customer progress, not AI usage.

The north-star metric is provider activation. Supporting metrics include median time to activation, paid conversion, support tickets per provider, CSAT, and a first-customer milestone. AI response volume is an operational metric, not the product outcome.

Product trade-offs

Use AI where flexibility helps; use rules where correctness matters.

For compliance-sensitive decisions, the prototype assumes deterministic rules and trusted content are the source of truth. An LLM can explain, summarize, and personalize, but uncertain or high-risk cases should route to a human rather than fabricate an answer.

04 · Technical Concept

Production architecture

A simple architecture for turning product events and trusted policy content into contextual onboarding guidance.

1. Product EventsProfile completion, uploads, inactivity, publication, conversion
2. Provider ContextStage, provider type, state, goals, blocker history
3. OrchestrationRules for critical logic + retrieval of trusted help content
4. LLM LayerPlain-language explanation, summarization, personalized next action
5. Outcome + HandoffTask completion, measurement, or escalation to a human
